Rules that apply in every region

  • Indian citizens

    No visa is needed for Nepal. Carry a valid passport or an Election Commission voter ID card, and show it at park and heritage site counters to pay the SAARC rate.

  • Other nationalities

    A tourist visa on arrival at Kathmandu airport and the main land borders, or applied for online before you fly. At last check it cost US$30 for 15 days, US$50 for 30 days and US$125 for 90 days.

  • Trekking with a guide

    Since 1 April 2023, rules require foreign trekkers in national parks and conservation areas to walk with a licensed guide booked through a registered agency. Enforcement has varied by region; ask us how it applies to your route and passport.

  • Restricted areas

    Upper Mustang, Manaslu, Nar Phu, Tsum Valley, Upper Dolpo, Humla and Kanchenjunga need a restricted area permit issued through a registered agency, usually for at least two trekkers with a licensed guide.

  • Climbing peaks

    Trekking and expedition peaks need a climbing permit on top of the area permits, priced by peak and season.

  • Kailash via Nepal

    The Chinese group visa and Tibet permits are arranged by the operator. Opening dates and rules for Indian passport holders are set each year, so confirm them before you book.
