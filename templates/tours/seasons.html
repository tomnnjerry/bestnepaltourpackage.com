{% icon "sun" %}Four seasons

Nepal's seasons in brief

{% icon "sun" %}Oct – Nov

Autumn

Dry after the monsoon, with the clearest mountain views of the year. Peak season for treks and heli tours, and the time of Dashain and Tihar. Book lodges, flights and helicopters early.

{% icon "rhododendron" %}Mar – May

Spring

Warmer days and rhododendrons in flower on the Annapurna and Langtang trails. The second trekking season; haze builds in the lowlands by May. Hot in Chitwan and Lumbini.

{% icon "snow" %}Dec – Feb

Winter

Cold nights above 3,000 m and snow on the high passes, but clear, quiet trails lower down. A good time for Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan and Lumbini, and for short treks such as Poon Hill.

{% icon "rain" %}Jun – Sep

Monsoon

Rain most afternoons, cloud on the peaks, leeches in the forests and flight delays to Lukla. Upper Mustang and Dolpo lie in the rain shadow, and the Kailash yatra season runs through these months.
